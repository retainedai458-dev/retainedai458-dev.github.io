Questions

Good to know

Is an exit reminder a call?

No. An exit reminder is a notification, shown as you leave a saved place with the checklist you wrote. Call style alarms are an option for Medicine and Everyday reminders only.

Does it work when my phone is locked or the app is closed?

Yes. Exit reminders and alarms are built to work with the app closed and the screen locked. For best results, allow the permissions Retained asks for and keep battery saving from stopping the app.

Why does Retained ask for location?

Only to tell when you leave a place you saved, and to pin where you left an item. It is used for your own reminders.

Do I need internet?

Reminders ring without internet and keep a history on your phone. You need to be online to sign in and to back up your data.

Which phones does it support?

Android phones. Some makers limit apps in the background, so Retained guides you through the few settings that keep reminders reliable.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. You can delete your account and everything in it any time from Settings inside the app.

Is Retained medical advice?

No. Retained is a reminder tool. It does not give medical advice, diagnose or treat anything. Always follow your doctor's instructions.
